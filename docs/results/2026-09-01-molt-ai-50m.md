# MOLT AI 50M BPE baseline and QA adaptation

## Pretraining gate

EXP-1012 trained a 49,883,520-parameter decoder Transformer with an 8,192-item
ByteLevel BPE tokenizer, FP32 master weights/AdamW moments, BF16 autocast, fused
causal SDPA and fused AdamW. Sequential sampling processed 8,835,072 tokens,
just over two passes through the 4,413,694-token training split.

The run completed in 225.257 seconds at 39,222 end-to-end tokens/s. Validation
perplexity over 196,608 tokens per evaluation decreased monotonically from
9,954.04 to 33.70. Peak PyTorch allocation was 4.745 GB, peak device-wide use
was 6.565 GB, and sampled GPU energy was 16.544 kJ. Fixed prompts generated
readable multi-sentence English, although story consistency remained imperfect.

## QA gate

The unmasked EXP-1013-v1 learned question formatting but failed the factual
answer. Answer-only target masking corrected the mechanism. The 200-step v2
checkpoint answered both “What did Roxy put under her feet?” and the held-out
paraphrase “What simple objects helped Roxy reach the top?” with big leaves.

Aggregate held-out answer perplexity reached its minimum at step 20 and worsened
thereafter. A separately persisted 20-step v3 control preserved the better
aggregate curve but failed the held-out paraphrase behavior gate. Therefore v2
is packaged only as a narrow factual specialist; v3 is retained as the honest
early-stopping control. Neither supports a general-chatbot claim.

## Decision

Accept the 50M infrastructure, subword-pretraining, coherent-generation, staged
checkpoint initialization and answer-masked SFT gates. Reject claims of broad
instruction following or production quality. Next work requires a larger,
diverse instruction set and automatic best-checkpoint selection using a
preregistered composite validation/behavior metric.
