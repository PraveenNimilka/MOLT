# EXP-1016 — sequence-length warmup

Date: 2026-09-02  
Decision: **rejected; Milestone 3 not promoted**  
Novelty: **none claimed**

## Hypothesis

A 128→256→512 context curriculum, while holding the model, sequential token
order, 8,192 tokens per optimizer update, AdamW semantics, total update count,
and context-512 validation objective fixed, would reduce time and GPU-board
energy to validation NLL within 1% of a fixed-context baseline.

This is a local reproduction of established sequence-length warmup, not a MOLT
invention. Li, Zhang, and He reported the original method at NeurIPS 2022.

## Infrastructure added

- `molt curriculum-benchmark` paired AB/BA runner;
- runtime-context mmap batches that retain one contiguous token stream;
- exact fixed-token geometry for every phase;
- full-context held-out evaluation and interpolated time/energy crossing;
- NVML board-energy and thermal-throttle capture;
- five-second stable start-temperature dwell;
- paired bootstrap intervals and automatic reject/promote gates.

## Results

Workload: 10,776,640 parameters, TinyStories BPE-8192, context 512, batch 16,
90 updates, BF16 compute, fused AdamW with FP32 parameters/state, RTX 4060
Laptop GPU. Candidate phases were 27 updates at 128×64, 27 at 256×32, and 36
at 512×16. Each phase therefore processed exactly 8,192 tokens/update.

| Seed | Time improvement | Board-energy improvement | Quality within 1% | Thermal safe |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1337 | +15.15% | +11.09% | yes | no |
| 2027 | −20.13% | −19.40% | yes | no |
| 4099 | +0.59% | +3.23% | yes | no |
| median | **+0.59%** | **+3.23%** | yes | no |

- Time improvement bootstrap 95% interval: **[−20.13%, +15.15%]**.
- Energy improvement bootstrap 95% interval: **[−19.40%, +11.09%]**.
- Every arm peaked at 75–76 C and reported NVIDIA software thermal slowdown.
- Candidate throughput ranged from 91,670 to 120,393 tokens/s, but was not
  thermally stable and therefore does not satisfy the combined gate.

Primary artifact:
`artifacts/molt-stream/experiments/exp-1016-20260902-084502.json`.

An earlier single-seed probe appeared to improve time by 25.25% and energy by
16.92%. The three-seed result demonstrates why that observation is not promoted.

## Thermal pacing ablation

A fixed 30 ms steady-duty pause reduced the baseline to 50,060 tokens/s. The
candidate aborted at 76 C after 42 updates. It failed throughput, reliability,
and thermal gates. Artifact:
`artifacts/molt-stream/experiments/exp-1016-20260902-084221.json`.

## Interpretation

Short contexts reduce attention work, but at this width the MLP and output
projection dominate enough that the saving is inconsistent. Different context
shapes also change kernel efficiency. The confidence intervals include large
regressions, so this is not evidence of a useful improvement.

## Exact reproduction

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\molt.exe --json curriculum-benchmark `
  --config configs\molt-stream-milestone3.json `
  --seeds 1337,2027,4099 --eval-interval 15
```

## Next algorithm boundary

Do not combine this rejected schedule with compiler or power tweaks to
manufacture a positive result. The next candidate should reduce dominant MLP
or vocabulary-projection work while preserving an exact or explicitly bounded
objective. Selective-token training, progressive layer dropping, mixture-of-
experts routing, randomized telescoping, and learned token skipping already
have prior art. A new proposal needs a narrower technical difference and a
gradient/update correctness kill test before training.
