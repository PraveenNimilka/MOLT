# MOLT name and branding policy

The MOLT name identifies releases produced by the MOLT maintainer. Copyright and
software-license permissions do not automatically grant permission to present a
modified distribution as an official MOLT release.

You may use the name truthfully to refer to this project, state compatibility,
link to the official repository, and preserve factual historical attribution.

Modified distributions must:

- use a name that does not imply they are an official MOLT release;
- state clearly that they are modified and identify their maintainer;
- avoid using official logos or release artwork as their primary branding; and
- avoid implying sponsorship, endorsement, certification, or support by MOLT.

Unmodified redistribution may retain the MOLT name when the original version,
license, notices, and source are clearly identified. Nothing in this policy
limits nominative fair use or other rights provided by law. No statement here
claims that MOLT is a registered trademark.
